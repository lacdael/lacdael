# Returns and Refunds Policy

**Last updated: 22 August 2026**

This policy applies to physical electronics, maker modules, embedded hardware and related products purchased from Wisdel.uk.

This policy does not limit your statutory rights under UK consumer law. In particular, goods must be of satisfactory quality, fit for purpose and as described.

## Cancelling an online order

If you are a UK consumer buying as an individual, you normally have the right to cancel an online order within 14 calendar days of receiving the goods, without giving a reason. You then have a further 14 days to send the goods back.

To cancel, contact us at **c.lacdael@live.co.uk** with your name, order number and a clear statement that you want to cancel. Please wait for return instructions before sending anything back.

Returned goods should be unused and in their original packaging, with all accessories, documentation and components included. You may inspect an item as you would in a shop, but you may be responsible for any reduction in value caused by handling beyond what is necessary to assess it.

Unless the item is faulty or was supplied incorrectly, you are normally responsible for the cost of returning it. We will refund the item price and the cost of our least expensive standard delivery option. Refunds will be made to the original payment method within 14 days of receiving the returned goods, or earlier where you provide evidence that they have been sent back.

The usual cancellation right may not apply to goods made to your specifications or clearly personalised. This does not affect your rights if a product is faulty, damaged or not as described.

## Faulty, damaged or incorrect products

Please contact us as soon as possible if a product:

- arrives damaged;
- does not match its description;
- is missing parts or accessories; or
- develops a fault during its expected working life.

Email **c.lacdael@live.co.uk** and include your name, order number, a description of the problem and photographs or a short video where useful. We may ask for basic troubleshooting information to distinguish a product fault from incorrect wiring, installation or use outside the product description.

Depending on the circumstances and your statutory rights, we may offer a repair, replacement or refund. We will provide return instructions and will cover reasonable return postage for products that are confirmed to be faulty or incorrectly supplied.

Do not return a product without contacting us first. For safety, disconnect electronic products from power before packing them and do not attempt repairs or modifications before contacting us.

## Pre-orders

Products marked as pre-order are not ready for immediate dispatch. If you need to cancel a pre-order before dispatch, contact us at **c.lacdael@live.co.uk** with your order number. If a pre-order is delayed or cannot be supplied, we will contact you and explain the available options.

## Exchanges

We do not offer direct exchanges as standard. Where an item is faulty, damaged or incorrectly supplied, we will normally arrange a replacement or refund after reviewing the issue.
